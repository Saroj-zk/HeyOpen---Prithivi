# Understanding $OPEN and Hey Open

Hey Open is the consumer AI product, while **OpenLedger** is the broader network and ecosystem context associated with the $OPEN token. The marketing pages discuss how AI usage, enterprise revenue, staking, compute, and potential token mechanisms could be connected.

## Separate product use from token participation

You can explore the AI service without assuming that holding or staking a token is required for every activity. The website promotes card and crypto payment options and also discusses staking-based benefits, but several token-related features are explicitly described as proposed.

## Core concepts

- **$OPEN:** The ecosystem token discussed on staking and tokenomics pages.
- **Staking:** Locking eligible $OPEN subject to applicable smart-contract and program rules.
- **Buyback and burn:** A proposed mechanism allocating part of available revenue to open-market purchases of $OPEN and permanent on-chain destruction.
- **Enterprise compute:** AI inference and related business services that may contribute revenue.

## Important status distinction

The tokenomics page uses present-tense language about automated buybacks and network operation, while the staking page marks several benefits as proposals and the site header says **Token Coming Soon**. Without verified contracts, transactions, and a final program specification, this documentation treats token economics as **described or proposed**, not as established current execution.

## Risks

Digital assets can fluctuate in value, and smart contracts or market mechanisms can fail. Do not treat strategy targets as guaranteed rewards or a subscription entitlement as live until it is activated in your account.
