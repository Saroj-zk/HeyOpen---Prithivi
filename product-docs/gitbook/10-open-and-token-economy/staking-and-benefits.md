# Staking $OPEN and proposed benefits

> **Availability:** The staking page includes proposed benefits, and the site states that the token is coming soon. Confirm current contracts, program terms, and availability before taking action.

The public staking page describes possible rewards, premium AI access, compute-credit collateral, developer benefits, and community participation tied to $OPEN.

## How the Pro threshold works

Hey Open Pro is granted while you hold **$1,000 worth of $OPEN** staked. The
threshold is denominated in dollars rather than in tokens, so the number of $OPEN
it takes to reach it changes as the token price changes. A position that qualifies
today can fall below the threshold without you selling anything, so check the
current requirement rather than assuming a fixed token count.

## Before participating

1. Verify the official contract address and network from trusted product materials.
2. Read current staking terms, lockup conditions, and withdrawal rules.
3. Understand how rewards are calculated and distributed.
4. Check whether Pro access depends on a continuously maintained balance.
5. Consider smart-contract, custody, liquidity, market, and tax risks.
6. Do not trust unsolicited links or ask anyone to handle your seed phrase.

## What the page does not establish

It does not provide a verified public proof in these sources that all described features are currently operational for every user. Avoid promising a fixed return, instant withdrawals, or uninterrupted premium access.

This page is educational and not investment advice.
