# Token utility and governance concepts

> **Availability:** Several items on the tokenomics and staking pages describe future or proposed network mechanisms rather than a confirmed service currently available in Hey Open.

The website outlines potential token utility beyond payment, including serving inference traffic, incentives for providers, governance participation, and agent payments.

## Concepts described

**Usage:** Tokens or related payment mechanisms may be used to support model or agent access.

**Provider participation:** Operators may be required to stake $OPEN to receive traffic, with penalties for certain failures under future program rules.

**Governance:** Token holders may participate in decisions regarding incentives, provider caps, treasury policy, or ecosystem direction.

**Rewards:** Providers could receive incentives for inference services or other approved work.

## What a finished governance reference needs

A production reference should publish verified smart-contract addresses, voting and proposal rules, quorum and execution thresholds, timelocks, upgrade authority, emergency controls, and an audit trail.

## Do not confuse goals with guarantees

Marketing terms such as immutable, fully automated, or governed on-chain should be used for a specific deployed implementation only when evidence supports them. The public token pages are useful for understanding design intent, but they are not a complete verified technical contract reference.
