# Choose the right AI model

No single AI model is best at every task. Hey Open lets you pick the one that matches your priority: speed, depth, code quality, context length, media format, or privacy route.

## A practical decision process

1. **Identify the output.** Do you need text, source code, an image, video, voice, or music?
2. **Check the input.** Does the model need to read images, files, or long documents?
3. **Prioritize speed or depth.** A fast model may be ideal for drafts, while a reasoning model may be worth additional latency for complex work.
4. **Review context size.** Long context helps with lengthy prompts, but can raise costs and does not guarantee better understanding.
5. **Check privacy properties.** Certain routes display labels such as Incognito, E2EE, or TEE. Review their actual meaning before submitting sensitive content.
6. **Check price and plan access.** Some options may be metered or require sign-in.

## Example selections

For quick rewriting, start with a smaller general-purpose model. For multi-file programming analysis, consider a code-focused model. For a document that must be processed in a hardware-isolated environment, inspect models that explicitly advertise TEE processing.

## When unsure, use Auto

Auto routing is intended to choose an appropriate model based on your request. This is useful for mixed workloads and token optimization. You can still select a specific model when you need particular behavior or provider characteristics.

**Important:** Selecting a model does not guarantee factual accuracy, legal suitability, or a particular data-retention arrangement. Verify the details associated with each route.
