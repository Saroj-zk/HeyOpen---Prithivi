# Music generation and usage

The Hey Open models catalog includes music as a generation modality and lists specialized providers. Use music-generation workflows for original soundtrack ideas, ambient beds, jingles, or other creative outputs where your selected model supports them.

## Write a music brief

Describe the mood, instrumentation, tempo, arrangement, duration, and intended use. Focus on musical characteristics rather than requesting a close reproduction of a protected recording.

**Example:**

> Create a warm, instrumental ambient piece for a product walkthrough. Soft piano, understated synthesizer textures, steady moderate tempo, no vocals, unobtrusive ending.

## Suggested process

1. Choose an eligible music model.
2. Review rights, cost, output, and availability details.
3. Submit a clear musical brief.
4. Listen for artifacts, unintended resemblance, abrupt endings, or changes in loudness.
5. Revise and export using supported controls.

## Copyright and commercial use

Ownership, licensing, content restrictions, and commercial permissions can differ by the provider and model. Review the applicable service terms before using generated material in public campaigns, products, or monetized media.

## A note on project continuity

A text model can help prepare the musical brief, while a music model performs generation. Do not assume all models automatically share audio files or have access to the full original prompt unless the application explicitly supports that transfer.
