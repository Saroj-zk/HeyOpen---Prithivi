# Create videos with AI

Video models can help turn a concept, script, or visual direction into generated motion. Hey Open's product guide illustrates a workflow in which a text scene becomes an image and then a moving shot.

## Prepare a strong video request

Include the subject, action, environment, camera movement, pacing, visual style, and any reference frame. A short, specific sequence is usually easier to evaluate than a complex multi-scene instruction.

## Example

> A slow cinematic push toward a mountain ridge at dawn. Low cloud cover moves beneath the ridge, soft natural light, a solitary figure seen from behind, realistic motion, steady camera.

## Suggested workflow

1. Draft a concept and shot list in chat.
2. Generate or upload a reference frame if supported.
3. Select a compatible video model.
4. Specify motion and scene constraints.
5. Review the resulting clip for continuity, artifacts, and unwanted content.
6. Iterate or combine the clip with separately generated narration or sound.

## Limits to expect

Duration, resolution, output format, queue time, pricing, and image-to-video support vary by model. A browser illustration is not proof that every listed model supports every operation.

## Sensitive footage

Only provide likenesses, voices, or footage where you have the necessary rights and permissions. Generated media may require temporary storage to render and deliver an output.
