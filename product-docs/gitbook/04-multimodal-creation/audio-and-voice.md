# Audio, voice, and transcription

HeyOpen lists audio-specialized providers in its model catalog and presents audio as part of its multimodal workflow. Individual capabilities may include speech generation, transcription, or other audio operations, depending on the selected model.

## Choose the right operation

- **Speech to text:** Convert recordings or speech into words.
- **Text to speech:** Produce spoken narration from written content.
- **Audio analysis:** Ask a compatible model to summarize or analyze supplied audio.
- **Sound design:** Create or process ambience and other audio where supported.

## Voice input versus generated voice

A microphone icon in the interface may support recording or voice-driven prompts. That is different from a model that generates downloadable spoken audio. Check the actual UI and current model description.

## A narration workflow

1. Draft a concise script.
2. Select an eligible speech-generation model.
3. Supply voice, language, pacing, and pronunciation instructions if supported.
4. Generate, listen to, and verify the output.
5. Make revisions to names, timing, and emphasis.

## Privacy and permission

Audio containing identifiable voices can be personal data. Do not clone a real person's voice or submit recordings without consent and necessary rights. Third-party media providers may have distinct processing and retention arrangements.

## If audio features are not visible

Check the current model catalog and your plan. The public page describes the capability family but does not document every production button or export format.
