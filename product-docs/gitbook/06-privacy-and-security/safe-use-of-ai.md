# Use AI responsibly and securely

HeyOpen offers access to powerful AI systems, including models with fewer content restrictions. Responsible use still requires appropriate judgment, permissions, and compliance with law and provider terms.

## Verify important outputs

AI systems can produce inaccurate statements, fabricated references, flawed code, or misleading summaries. Check consequential claims and use professional review for legal, medical, financial, safety, or regulatory decisions.

## Protect confidential data

Do not send passwords, recovery phrases, authentication tokens, private keys, or sensitive organizational files unless an approved workflow and processing arrangement explicitly permits the data. Redact personal information when it is not needed for the task.

## Review generated content

Confirm rights to source materials and output distribution. For media depicting real individuals or voices, obtain appropriate authorization. Review generated code for vulnerabilities, dependencies, and data exfiltration risks before deployment.

## Use agents carefully

An agent with access to external applications may send messages, modify records, or initiate irreversible actions. Restrict permissions, set spending or scope limits where available, and review consequential actions before authorizing them.

## About less-restricted models

The Terms clarify that an uncensored or less-restricted model does not authorize unlawful behavior, guarantee every prompt is accepted, or remove third-party restrictions.

## When unsure

Stop the workflow, inspect privacy settings, and contact support for account-specific questions. For business use, get the necessary security and legal approvals before uploading regulated or sensitive material.
