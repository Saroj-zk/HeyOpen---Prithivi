# Your data rights and privacy settings

Privacy rights depend on applicable law and the nature of the data involved. The Hey Open Privacy Policy describes possible rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, withdraw consent, and unsubscribe from marketing communications.

## Manage what you control in the application

Use the available Memory and Privacy controls to review saved context and select the sharing options appropriate for your work. Check the active model's processing route before uploading confidential information.

## Submit a privacy request

1. Send a request to **support@heyopen.ai**.
2. Explain whether you are requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction, or another supported right.
3. Identify the account involved without sending secrets or recovery phrases.
4. Complete any reasonable identity-verification process requested by support.
5. Keep the support correspondence for your records.

The Privacy Policy states that responses will follow applicable legal timeframes. It also notes that certain billing, fraud-prevention, legal, or dispute records may be retained even after deletion requests.

## Marketing versus necessary notices

Users may be able to unsubscribe from promotional communications. Administrative, account, security, or transaction notices may still be sent where legally permitted.

## Third-party actions

A privacy request to Hey Open does not automatically delete information lawfully retained by independent model providers, connected applications, payment processors, or blockchain networks. Those parties may have separate processes.

**Source of authority:** [Official Privacy Policy](https://hey-open.vercel.app/privacy-policy). This guide summarizes it and does not replace the policy.
