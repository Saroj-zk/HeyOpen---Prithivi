# Hey Open for enterprises

Hey Open presents a unified AI workspace and infrastructure layer for teams that need access to multiple models, governance, and privacy-focused processing options.

## Enterprise use cases

- Internal AI assistants for research and knowledge work.
- Developer and operations tools using unified inference APIs.
- Custom-branded AI products served under the customer's identity.
- Agent-enabled workflows with approved data and action permissions.
- Managed AI access across departments or business units.

## Product capabilities promoted on the website

The homepage describes model access controls, centralized user management, company-wide workspace access, usage and spend controls, audit visibility, and a company memory concept. The Enterprise page emphasizes custom-branded applications, prepaid compute, scalable inference, and optional compute-price protection.

## What requires a commercial agreement

Implementation details for tenant separation, data residency, audit exports, access control enforcement, encryption, SLAs, certifications, private deployment arrangements, and data processing addenda should be confirmed with the enterprise team. Marketing statements do not replace a security review or written contract.

## First step

Define your target workload, model requirements, expected volume, privacy constraints, integration dependencies, and budget. Contact **marketing@heyopen.ai**, which is listed as an enterprise contact on the public site, or **support@heyopen.ai** for assistance.
