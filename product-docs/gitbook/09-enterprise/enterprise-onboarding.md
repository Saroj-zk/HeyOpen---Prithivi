# Enterprise onboarding checklist

Use this checklist to plan a Hey Open enterprise evaluation. Exact provisioning steps and product configuration are defined through the commercial engagement, not through the publicly accessible app.

## Discovery

- [ ] Business problem, success criteria, and responsible executive identified.
- [ ] Workload types and projected request volume estimated.
- [ ] Models and modalities shortlisted.
- [ ] Required regions, providers, and privacy routes documented.
- [ ] Integration, identity, and security prerequisites collected.

## Security and legal

- [ ] Data classification and retention requirements agreed.
- [ ] External processors and permitted model routes approved.
- [ ] DPA, SLA, and commercial terms reviewed as applicable.
- [ ] Evidence for security certifications and controls requested.
- [ ] Incident notification and escalation procedures defined.

## Technical pilot

- [ ] API credentials issued through an approved process.
- [ ] End-to-end auth and access controls tested.
- [ ] Representative workloads evaluated for quality and performance.
- [ ] Metering and spending controls reconciled with expected usage.
- [ ] Unsupported operations and fallbacks documented.

## Launch

- [ ] Rollback and recovery plan prepared.
- [ ] Support ownership and escalation paths named.
- [ ] User training and permitted-use policy published.
- [ ] Monitoring, cost reviews, and periodic access reviews scheduled.

## Contact

Send enterprise inquiries to **marketing@heyopen.ai** as shown on the Enterprise page, or contact **support@heyopen.ai** for general product assistance.
