# Custom-branded AI applications

HeyOpen promotes **custom-branded AI solutions** in which a business delivers an AI product under its own brand while relying on HeyOpen's inference and infrastructure layer.

## Common delivery models

**Branded AI workspace:** A customer-facing or employee-facing experience with organization-specific design and approved model access.

**Inference API integration:** AI capabilities embedded into an existing application through a managed model-access layer.

**Agent-oriented application:** Task automation with approved tools, permissions, model routing, and human oversight.

## Suggested engagement process

1. Describe the target users and product outcomes.
2. Identify branding, authentication, tenancy, and deployment requirements.
3. Choose approved models and provider routes.
4. Define privacy, retention, and integration responsibilities.
5. Agree on metering, prepaid compute, service levels, and support.
6. Pilot with representative non-sensitive test data.
7. Validate performance, governance, and billing before launch.

## Important distinctions

A custom-branded solution is not necessarily a self-hosted model, a dedicated physical GPU cluster, or a fully on-chain deployment. Determine who operates each component and which commitments apply through the commercial agreement.
