# Plans and pricing

HeyOpen's public website promotes a free way to start and a **HeyOpen Pro** offer displayed at **US$12.99 per month**. The exact benefits, available plans, billing frequency, usage allowances, taxes, and eligibility should be confirmed at checkout.

## Free access

The landing page advertises a guest-first experience with no card needed to begin. Certain models, media tools, account features, or usage volumes may require authentication or a paid plan.

## HeyOpen Pro

The marketing page describes expanded access to premium AI models and supported text and image generation. It uses broad "unlimited" messaging, while the published Terms also describe credits, variable usage charges, restrictions, and provider-specific limits. **Use the in-app plan details and checkout as the authoritative description of your current entitlements.**

## Paid usage beyond subscriptions

HeyOpen's Terms allow API usage, additional credits, media features, agent actions, and other paid functionality. Different models can incur different costs. No static pricing table here is a promise of permanent rates.

## Before subscribing

1. Review the total payable amount and currency.
2. Check whether the payment renews automatically or requires manual renewal.
3. Inspect included credits, rollover or expiry conditions, and any overage rules.
4. Review model access and any media-generation limits.
5. Read the cancellation and refund terms shown during checkout.
