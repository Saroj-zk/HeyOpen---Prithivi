# Plans and pricing

Hey Open's public website promotes a free way to start and a **Hey Open Pro** offer displayed at **US$20 per month**. The exact benefits, available plans, billing frequency, usage allowances, taxes, and eligibility should be confirmed at checkout.

## Free access

The landing page advertises a guest-first experience with no card needed to begin. Certain models, media tools, account features, or usage volumes may require authentication or a paid plan.

## Hey Open Pro

The marketing page describes expanded access to premium AI models and supported text and image generation. It uses broad "unlimited" messaging, while the published Terms also describe credits, variable usage charges, restrictions, and provider-specific limits. **Use the in-app plan details and checkout as the authoritative description of your current entitlements.**

## Two routes to Pro

Pro can be paid for monthly, or unlocked by staking $OPEN.

| Route | What it takes | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subscription | The monthly plan price | Available now |
| Staking | $1,000 worth of $OPEN staked, which you keep | Staking is not yet live in the application |

Staking is not a cheaper subscription. The tokens stay yours and continue to earn,
but they are locked while staked, subject to an unstaking cooling period, and their
value moves with the market in a way a fixed monthly price does not. See
[Staking $OPEN](../10-open-and-token-economy/staking-and-benefits.md).

## Paid usage beyond subscriptions

Hey Open's Terms allow API usage, additional credits, media features, agent actions, and other paid functionality. Different models can incur different costs. No static pricing table here is a promise of permanent rates.

## Before subscribing

1. Review the total payable amount and currency.
2. Check whether the payment renews automatically or requires manual renewal.
3. Inspect included credits, rollover or expiry conditions, and any overage rules.
4. Review model access and any media-generation limits.
5. Read the cancellation and refund terms shown during checkout.
