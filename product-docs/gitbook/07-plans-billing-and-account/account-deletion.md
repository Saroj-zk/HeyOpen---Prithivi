# Close an account and request data deletion

If you no longer want to use Hey Open, you may wish to cancel paid services, revoke integrations, and request eligible account-data deletion.

## Suggested sequence

1. Review any active subscriptions and cancel recurring renewals if applicable.
2. Save copies of receipts or outputs you are entitled to keep.
3. Revoke application API keys and external integrations where supported.
4. Remove browser-local Universal Memory entries and other local site data when appropriate.
5. Contact **support@heyopen.ai** to request account deletion or exercise a privacy right.
6. Follow identity-verification steps communicated through official support.

## What deletion means

The Privacy Policy allows certain personal data, account records, usage information, or billing records to be retained where necessary for compliance, security, dispute resolution, or legal claims. It also states that deletion cannot necessarily undo prior processing by external providers.

## Public blockchain records

Confirmed wallet transactions may remain publicly visible and are generally not erasable through account deletion.

## Important distinction

Canceling your subscription does not necessarily delete your account. Deleting your account does not automatically clear all browser-local information on every device, and may not terminate external accounts you connected.
