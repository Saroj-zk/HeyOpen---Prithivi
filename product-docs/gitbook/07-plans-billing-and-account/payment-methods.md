# Card and cryptocurrency payments

HeyOpen's website promotes payment by card or cryptocurrency. These methods can differ in settlement, renewal, refund handling, and record visibility.

## Card payments

Card payments are generally processed by an authorized payment provider. The provider may collect billing and card information, while HeyOpen may receive transaction and subscription status information needed to administer the service. Check whether automatic renewal is enabled before paying.

## Cryptocurrency payments

Where supported, crypto payments may require a compatible network, token, wallet, and confirmations. A confirmed blockchain transaction can be irreversible. Crypto subscriptions may also require manual renewal.

**Before paying with a wallet:**

1. Verify the website and payment destination.
2. Read the displayed network and asset requirements.
3. Confirm the amount, network fee, and payment reference.
4. Approve only the required wallet action.
5. Keep the transaction hash for support if payment does not appear.

## Privacy implications

On-chain transactions can expose public wallet addresses and transaction histories. Even if AI conversations are private, blockchain settlement may be publicly observable.

## If a payment is pending

Do not repeatedly submit a new payment without checking the transaction and provider status. See [Billing troubleshooting](../11-help-and-troubleshooting/billing-troubleshooting.md).

**Important:** The exact supported assets and chains are not established by the public sources used here. Follow the live checkout instructions, not a guessed chain or address.
