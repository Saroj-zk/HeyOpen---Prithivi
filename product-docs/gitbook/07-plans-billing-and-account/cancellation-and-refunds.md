# Cancellation, refunds, and billing disputes

Your billing rights and obligations depend on the plan, checkout disclosure, payment method, and applicable law.

## Canceling a paid subscription

The Terms say you may cancel through available subscription-management controls or by contacting **support@heyopen.ai**. Unless otherwise disclosed or legally required, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period.

## Avoiding renewal charges

For a recurring plan, cancel before the renewal date shown in your account. Keep your confirmation and check whether access remains active until period end. Crypto subscriptions may use a separate manual-renewal arrangement.

## Refund requests

The published Terms say payments are generally non-refundable once the relevant service has been provided except where the applicable refund policy or law requires otherwise. The live Terms currently link to an unresolved refund policy placeholder, so exact time windows or eligibility should **not** be assumed.

## Reporting a billing issue

Contact support with the account identifier, purchase date, receipt or payment reference, amount, and a description of the problem. For a blockchain payment, supply the transaction hash and network. Do not share your card number, private key, or seed phrase.

## Typical cases

- Duplicate charge or unexpected renewal.
- Credits not appearing after a confirmed payment.
- Paid access not updating.
- Unexpected usage charge or estimate mismatch.
- Refund status not reflected by the payment provider.

Legal consumer rights take priority where applicable. For exact binding terms, read the [Terms of Service](https://hey-open.vercel.app/terms).
