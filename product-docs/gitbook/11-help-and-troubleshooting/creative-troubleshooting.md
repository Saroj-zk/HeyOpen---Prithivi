# Image, video, and audio troubleshooting

AI media models have different input requirements and output limitations. If an operation fails or the result is unsuitable, review the model's capabilities before retrying.

## My image is inconsistent

Make the prompt more specific about the subject, camera, colors, and composition. For complex layouts, divide the task into smaller iterations. Verify whether the model supports reference images.

## My video is too short or incomplete

Check the model's supported duration and workflow. A short generated clip may require further assembly or editing in another application.

## I cannot upload a reference

The selected model may not support your input type, or the file may exceed the active feature limit. Choose a compatible model and use a supported format.

## Audio output sounds wrong

Provide clearer language, pronunciation, pacing, and voice-style instructions. Check whether the selected model is designed for transcription or generation.

## Generation is slow or stalled

Media requests can take longer than short text completions. Check request status, current availability, and whether usage has been charged before submitting another attempt.

## Privacy and output access

Certain media workflows may use temporary storage. Save completed assets to your own authorized storage and review applicable retention and usage rights before publication.
