# Terms, privacy policy, and legal information

HeyOpen publishes a [Privacy Policy](https://hey-open.vercel.app/privacy-policy) and [Terms of Service](https://hey-open.vercel.app/terms). Both pages display an effective date of **27 October 2026** in the sources reviewed for this documentation.

## What the Terms cover

Eligibility, account duties, AI output limitations, model restrictions, subscriptions, payments, credits, APIs, agents, blockchain transactions, content rights, liability, and dispute arrangements.

The Terms state a minimum age of 13, with parental or guardian consent required for users under 18, and additional restrictions for certain mature-content features.

## What the Privacy Policy covers

Account data, technical information, prompts and outputs, third-party AI providers, Universal Memory, Compare, connected services, payments, retention, rights, and support contacts.

## Important publication note

The reviewed Terms contain unresolved fields for jurisdiction, registered address, several credit and billing policies, refund policy URL, and other legal specifics. These fields require legal review before the policy can be treated as production-final.

## Document hierarchy

This GitBook is educational product guidance. If a summary here conflicts with a binding agreement or a legally required notice, consult the authoritative terms and the applicable customer contract. Do not treat the help center as an amendment to legal terms.
