# Billing and credit troubleshooting

Billing issues often involve payment status, subscription entitlements, credit metering, or checkout-specific rules. Use your actual receipt and account state rather than assuming a payment succeeded when a wallet or bank reports only that it was submitted.

## Payment completed, but Pro is not active

Check the transaction status, account used during checkout, and applicable processing time. For cryptocurrency, confirm that the required network confirmations were reached. Contact support with the payment reference if the account does not update.

## Unexpected renewal

Review whether your plan was configured for automatic renewal and whether cancellation was effective before the renewal date. Crypto plans may use manual renewal, depending on checkout.

## Credit balance looks wrong

Check recent usage and the selected model. Consensus Mode, media features, and certain API or agent workflows may consume more credits than a basic text request. Metering can be reconciled after provider reporting.

## Credits disappeared or expired

The Terms describe different types of credits but still contain unresolved expiry and rollover placeholders. Request the applicable current credit policy rather than assuming a specific expiry rule.

## Refund question

Review the applicable Terms and any checkout disclosure, then contact **support@heyopen.ai** with the amount, date, and payment reference. Never send card CVV, seed phrases, or private keys.

## Needed for a support ticket

Provide a short description, time and time zone, affected account, plan, sanitized screenshots, receipt number, and transaction hash if applicable.
