# Contact support and report an issue

For product support, account questions, billing issues, privacy requests, or security concerns, contact **support@heyopen.ai**.

For enterprise or custom-branded AI inquiries, the public Enterprise page lists **marketing@heyopen.ai**.

## Help us diagnose the issue

Include:

- A short, specific description of what happened.
- The feature, model, or page involved.
- The approximate date, time, and time zone.
- Your browser and operating system when relevant.
- Any visible error message or sanitized screenshot.
- A payment reference, API request ID, or transaction hash when relevant.
- What you expected to happen and what occurred instead.

## Never include

Passwords, OTP codes, complete card details, wallet seed phrases, private keys, API secrets, or sensitive prompt content that is unnecessary for troubleshooting.

## For privacy rights requests

State whether you are requesting access, correction, deletion, or another right under applicable law. The team may ask for reasonable identity verification.

## For a suspected compromise

Use the official support address and take protective actions through the verified application. Do not trust unsolicited recovery links or people asking you to transfer funds to restore an account.

**Official references:** [Privacy Policy](https://hey-open.vercel.app/privacy-policy), [Terms of Service](https://hey-open.vercel.app/terms).
