Getting started

What is HeyOpen?

HeyOpen is a private, multi-model AI workspace. One app gives you 200 AI models, a privacy level you set per conversation, a memory that follows you between models, and tools for images, video and audio in the same place.

Available on: the web app. The app opens straight into a chat. You can browse the whole model catalogue without an account; sending a message requires signing in.

What makes it different

Every model, one subscription

Separate subscriptions to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and the rest come to about $165 a month. HeyOpen is $12.99.

Privacy you set per conversation

A filter in the header decides which models you may use, by how strongly each one protects the prompt.

Speed or cost, per request

A request mode on the composer biases the gateway toward the cheapest or the fastest providers.

One memory across models

Saved details about you are reused in new chats, whichever model answers.

Chat, image, video and audio

The same picker holds 64 text models, 35 image models and 34 video models, plus 12 single-purpose tools.

One API endpoint

An OpenAI-compatible endpoint reaches every model behind one key.

Getting started

Your first conversation

You do not need an account to look around. You do need one to send a message.

  1. Open the app Open the web app. It opens on the chat screen, with the composer in the middle.
  2. Set your privacy level, if it matters Click Privacy in the header and pick Min, Mid or Max. This decides which models you are allowed to use. Leave it on Min for ordinary work.
  3. Choose a model, or leave it on Auto The pill under the composer reads Auto. Auto picks a model per message. Click it to choose one yourself.
  4. Type your question Be specific about what you want back. Instead of “write a launch post”, try “write a 400 word launch announcement for enterprise IT buyers, with a headline, a subhead and three benefits”.
  5. Sign in when prompted Sending requires an account. Click Connect Wallet in the header.
  6. Follow up in the same thread Keep going rather than starting over. The thread holds the context, and you can switch models mid-conversation without losing it.

Starter prompts

Five chips sit under the composer on an empty screen: Weather, Code, Write, Analyze and Brainstorm. Each fills the composer with a starting prompt you can edit.

If the answer is not good enough

  • Too broad? Say what to keep, what to cut, and what a good answer looks like.
  • Misunderstood? Ask the model to state its assumptions before continuing.
  • Confidently wrong? Check it elsewhere, or ask a second model to criticise it. A second opinion sharpens scrutiny; it does not prove correctness.
  • Forgot something? The thread may have outgrown the model's context window. Restate the decisive requirement.
Good to know

A long thread costs more per turn than a short one, because the earlier turns are sent again as context each time.

Choosing a model

HeyOpen holds 200+ models. The picker in the app is the source of truth; this page is a snapshot taken on 4 November 2026.

  1. Open the picker Click the model pill under the composer. It reads Auto until you change it.
  2. Narrow the list Use the All / Text / Image / Video tabs, or type in Search models.
  3. Read the badges Each model carries badges for privacy, web access and restrictions. They are explained below.
  4. Pick one, or star it Click a model to use it. Click the star to keep it at the top of the list.

Chat

Privacy filter: Min, Mid and Max

This is the control most people miss. It sits in the header next to Memory, and it decides which models you are allowed to use at all, by how strongly the route protects your prompt.

Setting What it allows Text models left
Min Every model, whatever it is tagged. 64 of 64
Mid Private, TEE and E2EE models only. 10 of 64
Max TEE and E2EE models only, hardware-protected prompts. 9 of 64
  1. Click Privacy in the header Three settings appear, with a one-line description of the current one underneath.
  2. Pick a level Min for ordinary work. Mid when the material is confidential. Max when it must never leave an enclave in readable form.
  3. Check your model is still there Raising the filter removes models. If a model you were using has gone, this is why.
Important

HTTPS in transit is not end-to-end encryption, and Incognito is the weakest of the four badges: it hides who you are from the provider, but does not stop the provider keeping the prompt. Only E2EE means the prompt is encrypted before it leaves your device.

Good to know

Never put passwords, recovery phrases, secret keys or API credentials into a prompt, on any route and at any privacy level.

Request mode

Separate from privacy, the request mode decides which providers the gateway reaches for first. It sits on the composer next to the model pill, and reads Boost by default.

Mode What it does Use it when
Default Every model; the gateway picks the vendor. You have no preference.
Saver Cheapest providers first. The task is routine and you care about cost.
Balanced A balance of speed and cost. General work.
Boost Fastest providers first. You are waiting on the answer.
Good to know

Three text models and ten image models are badged Not in Boost and disappear from the picker in that mode. If a model vanished and the privacy filter is unchanged, check this.

Token optimisation

A request carries more than your message: the current instruction, the chat history, retrieved memory, tool definitions and any media. HeyOpen trims duplicated or irrelevant material and reuses context where it can, which the site describes as up to 60% fewer tokens billed on a typical example.

That is an estimate, not a guarantee on any given request. Trimming is not free of risk either: if correctness depends on a specific instruction stated earlier, restate it rather than trusting automatic selection.

Compare

Compare sends one prompt to several models and brings their answers together, so you can see where they disagree before you act.

Availability

Compare is available to members on the Pro plan and above and requires signing in. Opening it without an account shows a sign-in gate.

  1. Open Compare from the sidebar It carries a NEW badge in the Workspace group.
  2. Ask a question worth the cost Use it for a decision, a review or a trade-off analysis, not for everyday questions.
  3. Read the individual answers first The disagreements are the useful part. A combined answer can hide them.
  4. Verify anything that matters Agreement is not proof.
Cost

Every participating model consumes usage. One Compare question can cost several times a single-model turn. It is the easiest way to spend a lot of credits by accident.

Memory

Memory keeps useful details about you so you do not have to re-explain yourself to each new model. Reach it from Memory in the header.

Switch What it does
Save memories from chats Automatically saves useful details about you as you chat. Requires Use memory to be on.
Use memory Lets the assistant use your saved memories in new chats.

Turning Use memory off disables saving as well, because the save switch depends on it.

Important

Memory is sent to whichever model answers, so it travels under that model's privacy route. Raise the privacy filter before putting anything sensitive into memory.

Good to know

Memory is not chat history. It stores selected facts such as preferences, project details and decisions, not a transcript of every conversation.

Images

35 image models sit in the same picker as chat, under the Image tab.

  1. Select an image-capable model A text model cannot generate pictures. Check the Image tab.
  2. Describe the image, not the feeling Cover subject, intended use, style, composition, background, lighting and framing.
  3. Set dimensions only if the model exposes them Not every model lets you choose an aspect ratio.
  4. Assess before you regenerate Check anatomy, text rendering, logos and likeness. Every regeneration is charged again.
  5. Change one variable at a time Adjust the lighting or the composition, not both, or you will not know which change worked.

Plans

How you pay

Two ways to cover your credits, and one of them leaves what you hold untouched.

Pay directly

Card or crypto, whichever you already use. One bill either way.

Or stake instead

Stake USDC, ETH or BTC and the yield covers your credits. The position stays yours the whole time, and unstaking simply stops the credits.

Card versus crypto

Card subscriptions normally renew automatically unless the checkout says otherwise. Crypto subscriptions may require manual renewal, and a completed blockchain payment may be irreversible. Check asset, amount, chain and destination before paying, and never send a second transfer until support confirms the first cannot be reconciled.

Not a guaranteed return

What a stake earns moves with the market. Rates are not fixed and nothing here is a guaranteed return.

Cancelling

  1. Sign in with the identity that owns the subscription A different wallet or browser profile may show a different plan.
  2. Open Billing or Subscription and find cancellation
  3. Read the effective date Cancellation generally takes effect at the end of the current paid period, not immediately.
  4. Keep the confirmation

Cancelling the subscription does not revoke API keys, delete memory, close your account or unwind a stake. Those are four separate actions.

API

One endpoint reaches every model behind one subscription, built for use by people and by x402-ready agents.

1M+API calls per day
<200msmedian latency
5+model families

The endpoint is OpenAI-compatible, so an existing OpenAI SDK integration works by changing the base URL and the key. Set "model": "auto" to let the gateway choose.

curl https://api.openledger.xyz/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OPENLEDGER_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "model": "auto",
    "messages": [
      { "role": "user", "content": "Explain quantum computing" }
    ]
  }'

The site shows the same request in Python, TypeScript, TypeScript with the OpenAI SDK, and cURL.

Good to know

Log the serving model returned in the response where it is available, so a result can be traced back to the model that produced it. Two auto calls are not guaranteed to use the same model.

$OPEN and $HEY

$OPEN is the capital asset behind the product. The published model is that real usage generates revenue, a share of the net revenue buys $OPEN on the open market, and those tokens are burned.

Where the revenue comes from

# Line
01 Enterprise inference and API
02 HeyOpen subscriptions
03 Pay-as-you-go inference

What comes out before the buyback

  • Inference — the compute behind every call
  • Platform costs — the cost of running the platform
  • $HEY credits — compute already claimed
  • Reserves — held back, not spent

A portion of what remains funds the buyback, and the tokens bought are burned.

$HEY

$HEY is described as a compute credit: staked $OPEN mints it, and holding it pays out compute credit daily for any model on the network.

Not available in the app yet

The Staking page in the application reads Coming Soon. The marketing site describes staking, buybacks and burns in the present tense, but there is nothing to stake against in the product today. Treat the token material as a published plan rather than a feature you can use.

Troubleshooting

A model has disappeared from the picker

Two settings hide models, and they are the most common cause of confusion:

  1. The privacy filter. On Mid or Max only Private, TEE and E2EE models appear.
  2. The request mode. Models badged Not in Boost disappear in Boost mode.

I cannot send a message

Sending requires signing in. Browsing the catalogue does not. Click Connect Wallet.

Compare will not open

Compare requires signing in and a Pro plan. The gate states both.

The model forgot something from earlier

The thread may have outgrown the model's context window, or context trimming removed it. Restate the decisive requirement rather than assuming it is still in view.

A model gave a different answer than last time

If the model pill reads Auto, two requests can be served by different models. Name a model when you need a reproducible result.

A page says Coming Soon

See Not available yet. Model Factory, Staking, Voice Chat and every agent are unavailable in the current build.

Getting help

A Contact support control sits on the chat screen. Include the feature, the browser, the exact error text, the approximate time, the model used and a non-secret account reference. Never include a password, recovery phrase, private key or bearer token.

Frequently asked questions

HeyOpen

How is HeyOpen different from ChatGPT or Claude?

Those are models. HeyOpen is the workspace that brings 200+ of them together, with one memory and a privacy setting you choose per conversation, so you pick the right model per task instead of reorganising your work around one vendor.

Do I need a separate subscription for each model?

No. One plan covers the catalogue. What your account can actually run depends on your plan and provider eligibility.

Do I need an account to start?

You can open the app, browse all 200+ models and open the Image and Audio Tools without an account. Sending a message requires signing in.

Can I switch models without losing context?

Yes, within a conversation. Different models support different context lengths, so a very long thread may not carry over in full.

Privacy

What do Min, Mid and Max do?

They are a privacy filter, not a speed or cost setting. Min allows every model, Mid allows Private, TEE and E2EE models only, and Max allows TEE and E2EE models only.

Is Incognito the same as private?

No. Incognito means the provider does not receive your identity. It does not mean the provider stores nothing. Private means zero data retention, and TEE and E2EE are stronger again.

Which models are the most private?

The nine badged E2EE. Your prompt is encrypted on your device and decrypted only inside a verified enclave.

Does HeyOpen train on my chats?

HeyOpen states it does not train its own models on private chats or memory without separate authorisation. What a model provider does with a prompt is governed by that provider's own terms, which is why the privacy filter exists.

Models and usage

What is the difference between the privacy filter and request mode?

The privacy filter decides which models you may use. Request mode decides which provider serves them, biased toward cost or speed.

Which models can search the web?

The 24 badged Web. Everything else answers from training data alone.

How much does video cost?

Thirteen video models publish a price per second of output, from $0.02 to $0.28. Ten seconds therefore runs from $0.20 to $2.80.
